Pitching Dominance+ (PD+)

Historical dashboard for 4178 qualified pitcher-seasons from 1974-2026. PD+ combines ERA, K-BB%, BAA, HR/9, IP, and WPA using standardized component scores with era and park adjustments. These components are weighted 35%/35%/10%/10%/5%/5% in the PD+ formula.

All data was pulled from Fangraphs and Baseball Reference.

The adjusted component statistics are standardized against the qualified pitcher-season population. The weighted composite combines ERA, K-BB%, BAA, HR/9, IP, and WPA before being converted to a 100-centered final PD+ scale.

Interpretation: 100 is the qualified-season average; higher values indicate increasingly extraordinary overall pitching performance relative to the historical population.

Source workbook: Pitching Dominance+ (1974–2026). 2026 is provisional until the season is finalized.
