Pitching Dominance+ (PD+)

Historical dashboard for 4178 qualified pitcher-seasons from 1974-2026. PD+ combines ERA, K-BB%, BAA, HR/9, IP, and WPA using standardized component scores with era and park adjustments. These components are weighted 35%/35%/10%/10%/5%/5% in the PD+ formula.

All data was pulled from Fangraphs and Baseball Reference.
