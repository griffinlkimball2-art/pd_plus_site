Pitching Dominance+ (PD+)

Historical dashboard for 4178 qualified pitcher-seasons from 1974-2026. PD+ combines ERA, K-BB%, BAA, HR/9, IP, and WPA using standardized component scores with era and park adjustments. These components are weighted 35%/35%/10%/10%/5%/5% in the PD+ formula.

All data was pulled from Fangraphs and Baseball Reference.

Showing: 4+ hypothetical Cy Youngs

How it works: Each AL and NL season is treated as a hypothetical Cy Young Award, with the pitcher who had the highest PD+ receiving the award. Only pitchers with at least four hypothetical awards are shown. 2LG seasons are excluded because they do not represent a single league award. 2026 is included as a provisional prediction based on the current PD+ data.
